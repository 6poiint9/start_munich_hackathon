
# Lineguard Demo Video Skript (Sprache: Englisch)

Zwei Versionen für ein voice-over Skript: ein full cut (ca. 2:30min) und ein short cut (ca. 90 sekunden). Die visuelle Spalte ist eine Empfehlung, die den App-Bildschirmen entsprechen.

---

## Version 1: Full Cut (approx. 2:30 min)

Speaking pace is about 140 words per minute, and the text is around 340 words.

### 0:00 – 0:15 | Hook

*Visual: Cost curve, small dot on the far left, large one on the far right*

> "We are Lineguard, a launch control room for companies with fixed deadlines and many suppliers. One wrong number in the ERP. Nobody notices for days. And on Sunday, the production line stops. The later an error is found, the more it costs. Lineguard moves the moment of discovery to the day of the booking."

### 0:15 – 0:40 | Starting point and new file

*Visual: App dashboard, then the scan is uploaded, header turns red*

> "Soony is our first customer. At its Hamburg site, the art book for the Collector's Edition is booked in the ERP at 80,000 units. Now a scan arrives: a handwritten note from the supplier. The Watcher detects the new file immediately, with no restart. The Reader processes it, handwriting included."

### 0:40 – 1:05 | Anomaly and impact

*Visual: Finding view with evidence, file and location highlighted*

> "The note says 8,000. The ERP says 80,000. A source ranking decides: the handwritten note beats the booking. Lineguard knows the bill of materials, so it calculates what this means: 5,900 Collector's Editions are missing, and the line stops on Sunday. The page shows 'System says' beside 'secured.' Every number links to its evidence."

### 1:05 – 1:30 | Proposal and approval

*Visual: Proposal card with price, deadline, approver; click on "Approve"*

> "The Decision agent builds a plan: a reorder with price, deadline, and the person who has to approve it. The policy engine routes it by value and type to the right role. Nils approves the ERP correction. Paul Winter approves the reorder. Anything that commits money stays with a human."

### 1:30 – 2:00 | The agent stays on it

*Visual: Timeline view, emails in two languages, supplier reply, follow-up*

> "From here, the Action agent takes over. It drafts the email in the supplier's language and knows exactly who to contact. In this demo, mail is in dry-run or replay mode. After approval, the agent sends the email, matches the reply, follows up, or passes the case on. Every step and email is visible in the app."

### 2:00 – 2:20 | Result

*Visual: Side-by-side "€204,000 risk example" vs. €16,800 reorder; status becomes secured*

> "Finally, Lineguard prepares the ERP correction. The result: the error was caught on the day of booking. The Nowak reorder is €16,800. It replaces an example €204,000 risk at the line. Soony did not confirm the €204,000 value."

### 2:20 – 2:35 | Closing

*Visual: Logo and tagline*

> "This applies to any site where many suppliers converge on a fixed date: product launches, seasonal goods, series production, trade fairs. Lineguard: if something is missing, you know while you can still do something about it."

> "Today, Lineguard writes a correction proposal instead of changing SAP directly. A human approves critical corrections. The core stays the same. The next customer needs a package, not a new system."

### Notes

- Check that the numbers match your screens and say them exactly as shown in the app.
- "Prepares the ERP correction" is deliberate: today you generate a correction file rather than a direct SAP connection, so there's no contradiction if the jury asks.
- Say that €204,000 is an example or assumption. It is not a confirmed cost from Soony.
- Say that €16,800 is the Nowak offer in the demo, not a general result.
- Do not say that the Action agent sent live mail unless the demo is in live mode. The default is DRY_RUN.
- Do not say that an ERP correction is complete until the next ERP export confirms it.
- Show Soony as the first customer, not the only customer.
- If you have spare seconds, add one line on the architecture before the closing: "Five agents on one shared state. Every number comes from code. The LLM only reads documents and drafts messages."
- Spoken numbers: "eighty thousand," "eight thousand," "fifty-nine hundred," "sixteen thousand eight hundred euros," "two hundred and four thousand euros."

## Version 2: Short Cut (90 seconds)

About 195 words at roughly 140 words per minute.

### 0:00 – 0:10 | Hook

*Visual: Cost curve, dot on the far left vs. far right*

> "We are Lineguard, a launch control room for fixed deadlines. One wrong number in the ERP. Nobody notices. On Sunday, the line stops. The later an error is found, the more it costs. Lineguard finds it on the day of booking."

### 0:10 – 0:30 | Detection

*Visual: Dashboard, scan uploaded, header turns red*

> "Soony is our first customer. At Soony in Hamburg, the art book for the Collector's Edition is booked at 80,000 units. Then a scan arrives: a handwritten supplier note. The Watcher spots the new file instantly. The Reader processes it, handwriting included. The note says 8,000."

### 0:30 – 0:45 | Impact

*Visual: Finding view with evidence highlighted*

> "A source ranking decides: the note beats the booking. Lineguard knows the bill of materials, so it calculates the impact: 5,900 Collector's Editions missing, and the line stops on Sunday. The page shows 'System says' beside 'secured.' Every number links to its evidence."

### 0:45 – 1:00 | Approval

*Visual: Proposal card, click on "Approve"*

> "The Decision agent proposes a reorder, with price, deadline, and the right approver. Nils approves the ERP correction. Paul Winter approves the reorder. Anything that commits money stays with a human."

### 1:00 – 1:15 | Action

*Visual: Timeline with emails, supplier reply, follow-up*

> "Then the Action agent takes over. It drafts the email to the supplier in the supplier's language, matches the reply, follows up, or escalates to the next responsible person. In the demo, mail stays in dry-run or replay mode unless an approver releases it. Every email is visible in the app."

### 1:15 – 1:30 | Result and close

*Visual: €204,000 risk example vs. €16,800 reorder, status secured, logo*

> "The result: caught on the day of booking, the Nowak reorder is €16,800 instead of an example €204,000 at risk. The €204,000 is an assumption. It is not confirmed by Soony. Lineguard: if something is missing, you know while you can still do something about it."

> "Today, Lineguard prepares an ERP correction file. It is not a direct SAP connection. The next customer only needs a new package. The core stays the same."

### Notes

- Cut from the longer version: the ERP correction step, the "who else has this problem" list, and the architecture line. Keep those for the live pitch.
- Keep the first-customer statement, the "System says" versus "secured" statement, the human approval roles, and the ERP limit.
- Keep the note that €204,000 is an assumption and €16,800 is the Nowak offer.
- If you run over, shorten the Action section first, since the timeline visual carries it well on its own.
- Read it aloud once with a timer. If you finish under 80 seconds, you can slow down slightly for the numbers.
